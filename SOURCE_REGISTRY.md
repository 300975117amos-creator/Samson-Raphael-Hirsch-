**Verification legend**
- ✅ **Corroborated** — consistent across ≥2 independent reliable sources.
- 🟡 **Single-source / pending** — recorded but not yet independently confirmed.
- 🔴 **Disputed** — reliable sources disagree; see `RISKS.md`. Do not publish as
  settled.

## Corroborated facts (from ≥2 sources above) — safe to build chronology on

- ✅ Born **20 June 1808, Hamburg**.
- ✅ Died **1888, Frankfurt am Main** (exact day disputed — see R-H01).
- ✅ Rabbinic career, in order: **Oldenburg → Emden → Nikolsburg (Chief Rabbi of
  Moravia) → Frankfurt am Main (IRG, from 1851 until death).**
- ✅ Intellectual founder of **Torah im Derech Eretz**; leading figure of German
  Neo-Orthodoxy alongside Azriel Hildesheimer.
- ✅ Teachers: **Isaac Bernays** (Hamburg) and **Jacob Ettlinger** (Mannheim,
  ~1828–1830; ordination 1830).
- ✅ *The Nineteen Letters* (*Neunzehn Briefe über Judentum*) published **1836**
  under the pseudonym **"Ben Uziel."**
- ✅ Founded and edited the periodical ***Jeschurun*** (run-years disputed).
- ✅ Authored an influential **Pentateuch commentary** and a **Psalms commentary**;
  wrote **Horeb** (year disputed — R-H02).
